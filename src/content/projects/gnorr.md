The Great Naval Orange Race challenged our team to design, build, and program a miniature autonomous boat. It needed to carry a naval orange all the way around the reflecting pond at the University of Central Florida.

## My role

I served as chief CAD designer, programmer, and developer. The boat earned third place in UCF Engineering’s competition, which included 450 students.

## Designing a complete boat

The CAD files show a hull developed through several versions, from the original hull through HullV5. The parts and assembly files also document the propeller, motor mount, rudder, servo mount, and servo arm: the components that make a small autonomous boat a system rather than a single model.

## From CAD to the competition

The folder includes an assembled boat view as well as STL and 3MF exports for several components. We built and programmed the boat for the competition. The available files do not record performance measurements, so this case study focuses on the design evidence and confirmed competition result.

## What this project demonstrates

Turning an autonomous-vehicle challenge into a defined CAD system, carrying the design across multiple parts and hull versions, and contributing as a designer and programmer on a team project.
